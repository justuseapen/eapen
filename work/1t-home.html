01

The context

Private AI still needs somewhere to run.

1T Home is an owner-operated workstation for private inference. Its public product material describes a technically prepared home or studio, an approved model-serving profile, and a local API. The wider 1T offering extends to professional servers and private infrastructure. Explore the product range ↗

02

My role

I founded 1T Home to build local AI infrastructure. The product centers on an inference machine designed to run large models at home. The public product and its documented qualification process are the inspectable evidence here.

03

The engineering problem

Capacity is one part of the question. The published delivery process evaluates the actual checkpoint, precision, context, and concurrency, then proves the agreed serving profile on the unit. The site explicitly distinguishes a model fitting in memory from a promise about throughput. Read the qualification process ↗

Publicly documented product sequence

01. Qualify

Define the exact model and the workload it needs to handle.

02. Prove

03. Operate

Start in private mode, inside the owner’s boundary.

Engineering consideration

A successful response to one prompt does not establish a dependable service. Context size, simultaneous requests, and recovery after a restart all belong in the acceptance conversation.

04 / A useful lesson

Start with the work.
Then choose the machine.

The same principle applies to a small team’s automation: define the job, its limits, and what “working” means before choosing the technology. A clear acceptance boundary is part of the product.
