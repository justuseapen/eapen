01

The context

The right software starts with a specific job.

TradeCraft focuses on roofing: contractor discovery, estimating, and scoping. Its public approach combines structured data, computer vision, and language-model workflows around those tasks. Read TradeCraft’s product overview ↗

RooferRate provides a contractor directory, ratings, and reviews. RoofGuide presents a guided path through a roof claim and project scope. These are distinct products for decisions people already need to make.

02

My role

I founded TradeCraft. RooferRate and RoofGuide are the public products behind that work: focused tools for evaluating contractors and making sense of a roofing project.

03

The engineering problem

Useful comparison depends on the information underneath it. RooferRate states that its directory uses Google Places and public records, and that rankings are based on ratings and reviews rather than paid placement. That makes the basis for a recommendation part of the product’s public promise. Inspect the directory ↗

Engineering consideration

A polished estimate can hide an incomplete scope. Missing information should remain visible; an apparently precise output should not erase the uncertainty in its inputs.

04 / A useful lesson

A narrow tool can make
a consequential difference.

Start where someone must make a decision. Gather the relevant information, make its limits clear, and help that person move one step forward. That is a practical starting point for automation in any small business.
