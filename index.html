04 / Before we meet

A few practical
questions.

Who should be in the room?+

Leaders at an established company who want to understand how current AI tools can help their business. The visit starts with work they already know: how a task gets done, where it slows down, and who makes the decisions.

Do you come to our office?+

Yes. I travel to you and work with your team on site for 1–2 days. We’ll discuss your location, participants, and visit arrangements in advance.

What do we need to prepare?+

Start with a recurring task you’d like me to see. In our first conversation, we’ll discuss the people involved, the tools they use, and what we can work through together.

Will we have a finished automation?+

The workshop centers on demonstration and learning through your actual work. I may build the automation alongside you when the scope allows. A production build, integrations, or a broader rollout can be scoped separately.
