ILLUSTRATIVE EDITORIAL WORKFLOW

From a draft to a decision.

A fictional library announcement contains an unsupported claim. The system extracts the brief and flags the missing source for review.

  1. Request a source: hold the submission until the claim can be checked.
  2. Omit the unsupported claim: remove it and prepare a revised brief for the editor.

A complete sample goes straight to editor review. Nothing is published. The person sets the rule; the system applies it consistently.
