# Eapen Workshop art direction

An inhabited engineering studio: warm charcoal, oxblood red, ivory paper, precise diagrams, brass-gold annotations, and a quiet photographic surface. Large Newsreader typography provides the editorial voice; Archivo and IBM Plex Mono handle explanation and metadata.

The hero uses tactile, interactive paper artifacts to invite visitors into one publishing rehearsal. A source check becomes a human decision, then a visible reusable rule. Project stories provide separate factual evidence. Mobile receives a composed vertical version of the same material.

The studio background is illustrative AI-generated scenery. It is not a photograph of Justus's belongings or workplace. Existing approved portrait assets retain their recorded provenance in the September portrait audit. All meaningful text, controls, annotations, and diagrams are native HTML/SVG.

### Exact generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.

Asset type: atmospheric background photograph for the hero of a premium engineering and creative consulting website, landscape aspect ratio 16:9, high resolution.

Primary request: An extraordinarily beautiful, quiet overhead editorial photograph of a real charcoal-green architect's work surface, worn fine-grained linoleum, beautifully controlled raking afternoon light from the upper right. A human, tactile, precise engineering studio atmosphere. Large clear empty work area, approximately the central 80 percent of the image, for interactive documents and typography to be composited later in HTML.

Composition: true overhead camera with a slight natural lens falloff. The whole left half is dark, clean negative space. On the extreme lower-right edge place a single slim black fountain pen with small brushed brass details, lying diagonally, and two tiny silver paperclips. At the extreme upper-right edge a corner of a dark olive closed linen notebook barely enters the frame, with an unprinted cream paper corner peeking out. All props confined to far right edges, leaving center completely empty. No desk edges or surrounding room visible.

Lighting: soft directional sunlight casts a long translucent window-shaped pool of warm grey-green light across the right half of the otherwise dark surface. Authentic photographic shadows and subtle film grain, not a flat gradient. Warm restrained highlights, deep cool green-charcoal shadows, realistic surface scuffs and material fibers.

Color palette: near-black olive charcoal #151a16, muted sage grey, warm ivory details, small brass accents. Very dark left side suitable for cream text.

Constraints: no people, no hands, no screens, no logos, no visible lettering, no rendered website or interface, no titles, no graphic lines, no fake text. No loose sheets or objects in the center. No clutter. No oversaturated colors or cinematic orange teal. The image must feel like an expensive magazine still-life photographed with a medium-format camera; sophisticated, tactile, believable.
