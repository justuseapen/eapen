## Exact generation prompt

```text
Use case: identity-preserve.
Asset type: photorealistic founder headshot for Eapen Technology, a small independent technology consultancy.
Input images: Image 1 is the PRIMARY identity and current appearance reference, the man seated in the white dinner jacket. Image 2 is an older portrait of the SAME man, used only to help understand his natural smile and facial proportions; do not make him younger to match Image 2.
Primary request: Create a credible, professionally photographed head-and-shoulders portrait of this exact man. Preserve his recognizable face, current apparent age, skin tone, eye shape and color, hairline, nose, ears, jaw proportions and natural asymmetry. Dark short textured hair as in the current photo, clean shaven or the very subtle natural stubble visible in the reference.
Composition: vertical 4:5 photograph, head and upper torso, shoulders slightly angled, looking toward the lens, relaxed closed-mouth slight smile, approachable and thoughtful. Leave comfortable space above hair and below shoulders.
Wardrobe and scene: simple dark charcoal crewneck, no suit, no logos, understated real warm gray studio backdrop with very subtle texture.
Lighting: flattering soft window light, realistic skin texture and fine facial detail, natural color, mild depth of field, editorial portrait photograph made with an 85mm lens.
Constraints: authentic photographic result, no beauty-filter smoothing, no exaggerated jaw or muscles, no invented facial features, no glossy corporate stock-photo look, no text, no watermarks. Change the wedding context and outfit but preserve the subject's identity faithfully.
```

Only size and encoding were changed after generation, using Sharp to produce WebP assets. No facial retouching was applied separately. The approved production page uses this portrait.
